SF Symbols weight and scale

Owner: Apple

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sf-symbols

Learn: Nine weights matched to the system font; three scales relative to cap height. Match glyph weight to menu-bar text.

Do not transfer: Any SF Symbol shape in the app icon or logo (licence forbids it).

described, not shown
API page, no image. Status bar thickness value is not given; squareLength equals thickness (NSStatusItem).

Template image rule

Owner: Apple

Source: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appkit/nsimage/istemplate

Learn: Black plus clear only, alpha for opacity; the control recolours it.

Do not transfer: Colour, gradients or baked-in shadows in the menu-bar glyph.

Observed (viewed 2026-10-16): this file is Bartender's app icon, not a menu-bar glyph: a black plate with a white shirt front, black lapels and bow tie. One object fills about 70% of the plate.

The page gives a relative path, so the host here is resolved and unverified.

Bartender app icon

Owner: Applause Group, Inc.

Source: https://www.macbartender.com/

Learn: How a glyph sits among neighbouring menu-bar glyphs.

Do not transfer: The glyph itself.

Observed: two overlapping rounded tiles. A dark tile with small coloured dots sits front-left, and a bright blue tile sits behind it, top-right, with a white diagonal light. Depth comes from overlap, not from texture.

Apple app icon rules

Owner: Apple

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons

Learn: 1024 px layout, system rounded mask, square unmasked layers, few filled shapes, no own highlights or shadows, default/dark/clear/tinted.

Do not transfer: Pre-masked art, outline-only shapes, text, hairlines. The 824 px content size is not stated on the page.

described, not shown
Product page, no image.

Icon Composer

Owner: Apple

Source: https://developer.apple.com/icon-composer/

Learn: Layered Liquid Glass authoring with Default, Dark, Mono variants; needs macOS Tahoe 26.4 or later.

Do not transfer: Pre-rendered lighting.

Observed: a dark keycap-like plate with a glowing red rim. A red angular mark sits top-right and the word 'raycast' is on the key. A single accent colour carries the brand.
